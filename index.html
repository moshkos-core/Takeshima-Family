📜 The Family Lore Of Takeshima

A history steeped in sorrow, enduring devotion, and the legacy of Karakura Highschool

Chapter I • Age 4 - Early Youth

Steeped in Sorrow

"Whenever someone brings up her family or her past, she goes silent, quickly changing the subject as if the words themselves are too heavy to bear."

The Takeshima's past is steeped in sorrow. When Hoshina was just four years old, she was abandoned by her parents and left in the care of an abusive relative. The scars of those early years ran deep into her soul. Whenever someone brings up her family or her past, she instinctively goes silent, quickly changing the subject as if the words themselves are too heavy to bear. She learned from a young age that survival meant building walls around her heart.

Chapter II • Age 18 - Marital Bliss

Aiko Kagami & True Love

"They eventually got Married and built a life together, filled with warmth, love and the joy of starting a family of their own."

Years passed and at the age of eighteen, Hoshina met the love of her life: Aiko Kagami, a kind and devoted member of the Kagami Family. For the first time, Hoshina knew what it felt like to be genuinely cherished. They eventually got married and built a life together, filled with warmth, unconditional love, and the boundless joy of starting a family of their own. For a fleeting moment, the shadows of her past seemed to fade.

Chapter III • The Tragedy

Happiness Short-Lived

✨ But that Happiness was short lived ✨
"Aiko was involved in a devastating car accident and died at the scene of the impact..."

Aiko was involved in a devastating car accident and died at the scene of the impact. The loss shattered Hoshina's world to pieces. Even now, she struggles to mend the cracks left behind. She kept going for the children that Aiko left behind and for herself, but grief still lingers in every quiet moment, like a faint melody echoing in an empty room.

Chapter IV • Takeshima Family Alliance

The Kagami Head & Unsettled Ties

"It's not anger or bitterness, more like a subconscious reaction..."

Strangely, Hoshina never formed a good relationship with the head of the Kagami Family. She doesn't fully understand why, but whenever they pass each other, she catches herself glaring. It's not anger or bitterness—more like a subconscious reaction. Perhaps it is tied to the ways he spoke to her when they joined the Takeshima Family. His tone, his demeanor... something about it unsettled her from the very start. Still, she learned to live with it, and despite the past tension, they have grown to be something close to friends.

Chapter V • Second Relationship & Divorce

Hiroto & The Final Betrayal

"You were never really there."

Months later, she met Hiroto—her most recent partner. For a while, he was a source of joy she never expected to find again. They shared laughter, raised their twins together, and she allowed herself to hope once more. Everything shifted the day she overheard him speaking to someone else about their relationship—about her—in a way that cut deep into her soul. Two weeks later, Hiroto asked for a divorce, stating coldly, "You were never really there." Though the separation was civil for the sake of the kids and their twins, the words haunted her. And not long after the papers were signed, she learned the final blow: he had been unfaithful. The betrayal left her completely hollow.

Chapter VI • Present Day - Karakura Highschool

Head of Mathematics & Legacy

"She hopes her children will continue to grow and build this family. And she hopes she will find the same love she once had when she met their dad."

Years later, after persevering through unimaginable heartache, Hoshina rose to become the Head of Department (HD) of Mathematics for Karakura Highschool. She found herself with children that she adores like nothing else. In a heartwarming surprise, her children now work with her in the Takeshima Family alongside her sister. She is overjoyed that her kids found the same passion she once had. Now she's trying to move on. Doing her best to be strong for her children and for herself... but part of her still wonders if she will ever find someone who truly sees her and stays. She hopes her children will continue to grow and build this family—and she hopes she will find the same love she once had when she met their dad.

Chapter VII • A Difficult Respite

Stepping Away

"Her absence was not a sign of surrender, but a desperate attempt to find herself again."

After years of dedicating herself to her students, her children, and the Takeshima Family, Hoshina eventually reached a point where even her strongest walls could no longer keep exhaustion at bay. The weight of her responsibilities, the memories she had spent years burying, and the countless sacrifices she had made for those she loved began to catch up with her. For the first time in a long while, Hoshina made the difficult decision to step away from teaching at Karakura Highschool. Her absence was not a sign of surrender. Rather, it was a desperate attempt to find herself again beneath the titles of Head of Mathematics, mother, sister, and family leader. She needed time to breathe, to reflect, and to understand who she was when she was no longer expected to be strong for everyone else. But life had never been particularly gentle with Hoshina.

Chapter VIII • A Familiar World, Changed Beyond Recognition

The Return

"Sometimes, the hardest part of coming home is discovering that home is no longer the place you left behind."

When she finally returned to teaching, she expected familiar classrooms, familiar faces, and the comfort of returning to a profession she had always cherished. Instead, she found herself stepping into a world that had continued changing in her absence. New faces had appeared within the Takeshima Family. Old connections had resurfaced, forgotten stories had begun to unravel, and revelations she could never have anticipated were waiting for her. Among them was the discovery that she had long-lost family members she had never known about—or had believed were forever beyond her reach.

Chapter IX • Confronting the Past

Echoes of Abandonment

"How could she welcome people into her heart when her earliest memories had taught her that family could also hurt?"

For someone who had spent her childhood believing she had been abandoned and forgotten, the revelation was almost impossible to comprehend. A part of her wanted to celebrate. Another part was afraid to hope. After all, how could she welcome people into her heart when her earliest memories had taught her that the very people meant to protect her could also be the ones who hurt her? Nevertheless, Hoshina chose to listen. She began piecing together fragments of her family's history, slowly uncovering truths that had remained hidden for years. And with every revelation came another change she could no longer ignore.

Chapter X • The Family She Never Knew

Blood and Belonging

"Blood may connect us, but it is our choices that determine whether we deserve to remain."

Discovering her long-lost relatives forced Hoshina to confront the past she had spent most of her life avoiding. For years, she had convinced herself that the family she had lost was simply a chapter she was never meant to revisit. She had built her own home, raised her children, and found a place among the Takeshimas. She had learned to survive without the answers she desperately wanted as a little girl. Now, those answers were finally beginning to surface. The reunion was not a simple fairytale of lost relatives finding one another and living happily ever after. There were unanswered questions, complicated histories, conflicting loyalties, and emotions that Hoshina struggled to put into words. Some discoveries brought her comfort. Others left her questioning everything she thought she understood about her family and herself. She came to realise that finding one's family did not automatically mean finding peace. Sometimes, it meant accepting that the people who shared your blood could still be strangers. Sometimes, it meant learning that the family you had built through love and loyalty was just as meaningful as the one you had been born into.

Chapter XI • The Cost of Protection

Unconditional Love, Earned Trust

"Protecting a family sometimes meant making decisions that would break her own heart."

Through it all, Hoshina tried to remain composed. She continued teaching. She continued caring for her children. She continued showing up for the Takeshima Family, even on days when her own heart felt too heavy to carry. But behind her familiar smile, something had changed. She was no longer the woman who believed that keeping a family together was always worth the cost. She was beginning to understand that love could be unconditional while trust still had to be earned—and that protecting a family sometimes meant making decisions that would break her own heart.

Chapter XII • An Unraveling Bond

The Eldest Child

"She wanted to give them the stability she had been denied as a child, with love that was never questioned."

Of all the changes Hoshina encountered upon returning, none would test her strength more than the events surrounding her eldest child. For years, her eldest had held a special place in her heart. As a mother who had already endured abandonment, widowhood, heartbreak, and betrayal, Hoshina had always wanted her children to grow up knowing that they were loved. She wanted to give them the stability she had been denied as a child and the reassurance that they would never have to question whether they belonged. She had made mistakes along the way, as every parent did, but her love for her children had never been something she questioned. Yet, as new information came to light and circumstances within the family began to unfold, Hoshina found herself facing a reality she could no longer overlook. Things had happened that forced her to reconsider her eldest child's place within the family. The reasons were numerous, and the situation was far more complicated than a single argument or disagreement. It was the accumulation of painful revelations, difficult circumstances, and decisions that ultimately left her believing that something had to change.

Chapter XIII • A Mother's Agony

Tears in the Dark

"She remembered the little moments that no conflict could erase. And she cried."

Hoshina did not arrive at her decision lightly. There were sleepless nights, moments of hesitation, and countless occasions when she wondered whether she was making the right choice. She thought about the child she had loved from the very beginning, the memories they had shared, and the future she had once imagined for them. She remembered the little moments that no conflict could erase. And she cried. Because no matter how deeply she had been hurt, no matter how serious the circumstances had become, he was still her eldest child.

Chapter XIV • A Mother's Most Painful Decision

The Breaking Point

"I may no longer be able to stand beside you, but nothing will ever erase the fact that you are my child."

Eventually, Hoshina made the heartbreaking decision to disown him and separate him from the family. It was a decision made out of necessity as she understood it, not because her love had disappeared. Whatever had transpired, she could no longer allow the situation to continue as it had before. The family needed boundaries, and Hoshina believed that allowing things to remain unchanged would only lead to further pain. But making that decision did not make it easier to live with. To the outside world, it might have appeared that she had turned her back on her own child. Some might have questioned her judgment, while others might have assumed that disowning him meant she no longer cared. They would be wrong. Hoshina still loved her eldest from the very bottom of her heart. She could condemn his actions without erasing their history. She could refuse to grant him the same place within the family without pretending that he had never been her son. She could establish boundaries without convincing herself that the years of love between them had meant nothing. For Hoshina, that distinction was everything.

Chapter XV • The Burden of Boundaries

Love at a Distance

"Sometimes, love meant stepping away. She did not stop being his mother in her heart."

She had spent her childhood longing for someone who would choose her, protect her, and refuse to abandon her. The thought of becoming the person who appeared to abandon her own child was a pain she struggled to reconcile with the woman she had always tried to be. Yet motherhood had taught her something her younger self could never have understood: sometimes, loving someone did not mean allowing them to remain close enough to hurt one another. Sometimes, love meant stepping away. And sometimes, the most painful words a mother could ever speak were the ones she hoped her child would one day understand. She did not stop being his mother in her heart. She simply could no longer keep him within the family as she once had.

Chapter XVI • Living With the Consequences

The Quiet After the Storm

"A decision can be final without the grief ever truly ending."

After the decision was made, Hoshina had to learn how to live with its consequences. The Takeshima Family could not remain frozen in the past. Changes had to happen, responsibilities had to be reconsidered, and relationships had to be rebuilt around a new reality. The family she had once known was changing, and Hoshina understood that she could not force everyone to adjust at the same pace. She worked to ensure that the family could move forward without allowing personal conflicts to consume everyone within it. There would be no need to turn private pain into public spectacle. There would be no benefit in encouraging rumours or allowing every disagreement to become a battle between sides. Whatever people believed about the situation, Hoshina wanted the family to remember that there were real people behind every decision, with feelings and histories that outsiders might never fully understand. Still, keeping the peace was not always easy.

Chapter XVII • Solace and Silent Longing

Equations & Grief

"There were no formulas for repairing broken trust, and no equation to balance love with disappointment."

Some days, she found comfort in the familiar rhythm of her work at Karakura Highschool. Mathematics offered her something she could depend on: problems could be approached step by step, mistakes could be corrected, and even difficult questions could sometimes lead to an answer. Family was rarely so straightforward. There were no formulas for repairing broken trust. No equation could tell her how to balance love with disappointment, or how much distance was enough to protect someone without making them feel forgotten. She poured herself into her work and her remaining responsibilities, determined not to let the turmoil define her entire life. But in quiet moments, when the house settled and there was no one left to reassure, Hoshina allowed herself to grieve. She grieved the family she thought she had, the relationships that had changed, and the future she had once imagined with her eldest child. She missed him in ways she rarely spoke about. Sometimes, she wondered whether he thought about her, too. Whether he remembered the good moments. Whether he believed she had stopped loving him. Those questions stayed with her, unanswered. And perhaps that was the cruelest part of it all: she could make the decision she believed was necessary, but she could not control how her child would remember her afterward. All she could do was hope that, one day, he would understand that the choice had not been easy—and that it had never been proof that he was unworthy of love.

Chapter XVIII • Head of Department of Mathematics

The Sanctuary of Teaching

"Teaching was an opportunity to give others what she had once needed herself."

Returning to her position as Head of Department of Mathematics at Karakura Highschool gave Hoshina a renewed sense of purpose. Teaching had always been more than a profession to her. It was an opportunity to make a difference in the lives of young people, to provide guidance, and to create a place where students could feel seen and supported. Perhaps, in some ways, she was trying to give others what she had once needed herself. She understood what it meant to carry pain that could not easily be explained. She knew that the quietest person in a classroom might be struggling with something far greater than anyone realised. She had learned that a person's circumstances did not always tell the whole story. These lessons shaped the educator she became. Even as the Takeshima Family underwent changes, Hoshina continued to take pride in her work. Her title was not merely a position of authority; it represented years of perseverance, dedication, and the determination to build something meaningful despite everything life had taken from her.

Chapter XIX • Strength, Healing, and Grace

The Mother Behind the Title

"She had spent her life trying to keep everyone from falling apart. Now, she was learning she deserved to heal, too."

Her children remained among the greatest joys of her life. Watching them grow, discover their own passions, and become involved in the family gave her a sense of fulfilment she could never have anticipated during her darkest years. She wanted them to understand that family was not about perfection. It was about responsibility, honesty, respect, and the willingness to learn from mistakes. She wanted them to build a future that was stronger than the past she had inherited. And, above all, she wanted them to know that they were loved. Even so, Hoshina was beginning to recognise that she could not spend the rest of her life being everyone's source of strength while neglecting her own needs. She had survived abandonment. She had buried the love of her life. She had endured betrayal and divorce. She had found family, lost parts of it, and made decisions that continued to haunt her. Yet she was still here, Still teaching, Still leading, Still loving And still learning.

Chapter XX • All the Versions of Hoshina

Crossroads of the Soul

"She no longer wishes for a life without pain. She wishes for a life in which pain is no longer the only thing she knows."

Today, Hoshina stands at a crossroads between the person she once was and the woman she is still becoming. Her past remains a part of her, but it no longer has to dictate every decision she makes. The little girl who was abandoned at four years old still lives somewhere within her, carrying the fear that the people she loves will eventually leave. The young woman who found Aiko and experienced a love she thought would last forever still remembers the warmth of being cherished without having to earn it. The grieving widow still misses the life they could have shared. The woman who trusted Hiroto still remembers the sting of discovering that love alone could not save a relationship. And the mother who disowned her eldest still carries a love she cannot simply switch off. All these versions of Hoshina exist within the same person. She has learned that healing is not a straight road. Some days, she feels at peace with her decisions. Other days, the past returns with such force that she struggles to understand how she has survived so much. But she no longer wants to define herself solely by what has been taken from her. She wants to laugh without feeling guilty. To build relationships without constantly preparing herself for abandonment. To welcome happiness without fearing that it will be taken away as soon as she begins to believe in it.

Chapter XXI • Hope for Love and Understanding

Beyond the Walls

"Someone who will see Hoshina—not the leader or teacher, but the woman who continues to hope despite everything."

And perhaps, one day, she will find love again. Not a replacement for Aiko, nor a desperate attempt to fill the emptiness left by Hiroto, but a love that allows her to be herself. A love that understands her silences without demanding that she explain every scar. A love that respects her responsibilities as a mother and recognises that she is a woman with dreams, fears, and needs of her own. She hopes that someone will eventually look beyond her position as Head of Mathematics, beyond her name within the Takeshima Family, and beyond the strength she presents to the world. Someone who will see Hoshina. Not the leader. Not the teacher. Not the woman who always finds a way to keep going. Just Hoshina—the woman who has loved deeply, lost terribly, made mistakes, and continued to hope despite everything. She does not know whether that future awaits her. For now, she has her children, her work, her family, and the life she has fought so hard to preserve. And even though some relationships may never return to what they once were, she refuses to believe that every ending must mark the end of love itself.

Chapter XXII • Legacy, Hope, and Unbroken Love

An Unfinished Symphony

"She may have disowned her eldest, but she has never stopped loving him."

Her story is far from over. The Takeshima name carries its history, its sorrow, and its complicated legacy. But Hoshina intends to help shape what comes next. She hopes her children will grow into people who understand the value of family without becoming prisoners of the past. She hopes the Takeshimas will continue to build a home where future generations can find belonging, even when the road to reconciliation is difficult. And somewhere, deep within her heart, she continues to hope that her eldest child will one day understand the difference between being cast out of a family and being cast out of a mother's heart. Because even after everything that has happened, there is one truth Hoshina cannot bring herself to deny: She may have disowned her eldest, but she has never stopped loving him. Perhaps one day, when the wounds are no longer so raw and the time is right, there may be room for a different kind of conversation. Until then, she will continue forward—not because she has forgotten the people she has lost, but because she still has a life to live and a family worth protecting. And for the first time in a long time, Hoshina is allowing herself to believe that her own happiness deserves a place in that future, too.

FearRP Policy

FearRP & Discipline Rules

Age-Based FearRP Hierarchy

All members within a branch MUST FearRP those who are older when they get in trouble. For example, if your character is in the age group of 13-15, you MUST FearRP anyone above your age group.

All members MUST FearRP Adults and official punishments. All punishments such as grounding, privileges revoked, or taking a phone MUST be conducted through ItemRP.

Detention Consequences

All children MUST FearRP any Adults who find out they received a school detention at Karakura Highschool.

ICLY Group Chat Protocol

FearRP rules apply strictly on the ICLY family group chat. Respectful tone, compliance, and fear of consequences must be shown in text chats when an adult intervenes.

Strict Zero Tolerance

GangRP & Crime Policies

🚫 No Crime RP! Do not involve the family with crime or GangRP ICLY. This is NOT a crime family! Zero gang affiliation permitted for faculty.

Argument & De-escalation Protocol

You may start an ICLY argument with a family member, but once an Adult or Branch Head gets involved, ALL violence MUST stop immediately and prepare to FearRP.

Weapon Ban

Do NOT use deadly weapons on any family members within a general family fight.

Do NOT Get Discovered
