📜 The Family Lore Of Takeshima

A history steeped in sorrow, enduring devotion, and the legacy of Karakura Highschool

Chapter I • Age 4 - Early Youth

Steeped in Sorrow

"Whenever someone brings up her family or her past, she goes silent, quickly changing the subject as if the words themselves are too heavy to bear."

The Takeshima's past is steeped in sorrow. When Hoshina was just four years old, she was abandoned by her parents and left in the care of an abusive relative. The scars of those early years ran deep into her soul. Whenever someone brings up her family or her past, she instinctively goes silent, quickly changing the subject as if the words themselves are too heavy to bear. She learned from a young age that survival meant building walls around her heart.

Chapter II • Age 18 - Marital Bliss

Aiko Kagami & True Love

"They eventually got Married and built a life together, filled with warmth, love and the joy of starting a family of their own."

Years passed and at the age of eighteen, Hoshina met the love of her life: Aiko Kagami, a kind and devoted member of the Kagami Family. For the first time, Hoshina knew what it felt like to be genuinely cherished. They eventually got married and built a life together, filled with warmth, unconditional love, and the boundless joy of starting a family of their own. For a fleeting moment, the shadows of her past seemed to fade.

Chapter III • The Tragedy

Happiness Short-Lived

✨ But that Happiness was short lived ✨
"Aiko was involved in a devastating car accident and died at the scene of the impact..."

Aiko was involved in a devastating car accident and died at the scene of the impact. The loss shattered Hoshina's world to pieces. Even now, she struggles to mend the cracks left behind. She kept going for the children that Aiko left behind and for herself, but grief still lingers in every quiet moment, like a faint melody echoing in an empty room.

Chapter IV • Takeshima Family Alliance

The Kagami Head & Unsettled Ties

"It's not anger or bitterness, more like a subconscious reaction..."

Strangely, Hoshina never formed a good relationship with the head of the Kagami Family. She doesn't fully understand why, but whenever they pass each other, she catches herself glaring. It's not anger or bitterness—more like a subconscious reaction. Perhaps it is tied to the ways he spoke to her when they joined the Takeshima Family. His tone, his demeanor... something about it unsettled her from the very start. Still, she learned to live with it, and despite the past tension, they have grown to be something close to friends.

Chapter V • Second Relationship & Divorce

Hiroto & The Final Betrayal

"You were never really there."

Months later, she met Hiroto—her most recent partner. For a while, he was a source of joy she never expected to find again. They shared laughter, raised their twins together, and she allowed herself to hope once more. Everything shifted the day she overheard him speaking to someone else about their relationship—about her—in a way that cut deep into her soul. Two weeks later, Hiroto asked for a divorce, stating coldly, "You were never really there." Though the separation was civil for the sake of the kids and their twins, the words haunted her. And not long after the papers were signed, she learned the final blow: he had been unfaithful. The betrayal left her completely hollow.

Chapter VI • Present Day - Karakura Highschool

Head of Mathematics & Legacy

"She hopes her children will continue to grow and build this family. And she hopes she will find the same love she once had when she met their dad."

Years later, after persevering through unimaginable heartache, Hoshina rose to become the Head of Department (HD) of Mathematics for Karakura Highschool. She found herself with children that she adores like nothing else. In a heartwarming surprise, her children now work with her in the Takeshima Family alongside her sister. She is overjoyed that her kids found the same passion she once had. Now she's trying to move on. Doing her best to be strong for her children and for herself... but part of her still wonders if she will ever find someone who truly sees her and stays. She hopes her children will continue to grow and build this family—and she hopes she will find the same love she once had when she met their dad.

🌸 TAKESHIMA FAMILY LORE

The Story of Hoshina Takeshima

"Resilience through sorrow • Unity in passion • Karakura Highschool Legacy"

The Takeshima's past is steeped in sorrow. When Hoshina was just four years old, she was abandoned by her parents and left in the care of an abusive relative. Whenever someone brings up her family or her past, she instinctively goes silent. She learned from a young age that survival meant building walls around her heart.

Years passed and at the age of eighteen, Hoshina met the love of her life: Aiko Kagami. They eventually got married and built a life together, filled with warmth, unconditional love, and the boundless joy of starting a family of their own.

Aiko was involved in a devastating car accident and died at the scene. The loss shattered Hoshina's world to pieces. She kept going for the children that Aiko left behind.

Strangely, Hoshina never formed a good relationship with the head of the Kagami Family. It's not anger or bitterness—more like a subconscious reaction. Despite the past tension, they have grown to be something close to friends.

Months later, she met Hiroto. Two weeks after she overheard him speaking poorly of her, Hiroto asked for a divorce, stating coldly, "You were never really there." And not long after the papers were signed, she learned he had been unfaithful.

Years later, Hoshina rose to become the Head of Department of Mathematics for Karakura Highschool. Her children now work with her in the Takeshima Family. She hopes her children will continue to grow and build this family—and she hopes she will find the same love she once had when she met their dad.

⚖️ Takeshima Family Rules

FearRP Policy

FearRP & Discipline Rules

Age-Based FearRP Hierarchy

All members within a branch MUST FearRP those who are older when they get in trouble. For example, if your character is in the age group of 13-15, you MUST FearRP anyone above your age group.

Adults & ItemRP Punishments

All members MUST FearRP Adults and official punishments. All punishments such as grounding, privileges revoked, or taking a phone MUST be conducted through ItemRP.

Detention Consequences

All children MUST FearRP any Adults who find out they received a school detention at Karakura Highschool.

ICLY Group Chat Protocol

FearRP rules apply strictly on the ICLY family group chat. Respectful tone, compliance, and fear of consequences must be shown in text chats when an adult intervenes.

Strict Zero Tolerance

GangRP & Crime Policies

🚫 No Crime RP! Do not involve the family with crime or GangRP ICLY. This is NOT a crime family! Zero gang affiliation permitted for faculty.

Zero Faculty Gang Affiliation

Do NOT involve any Family faculty members, branch heads, teachers, or employees with GangRP or CrimeRP under any circumstances.

Argument & De-escalation Protocol

You may start an ICLY argument with a family member, but once an Adult or Branch Head gets involved, ALL violence MUST stop immediately and prepare to FearRP.

Weapon Ban

Do NOT use deadly weapons on any family members within a general family fight.

Do NOT Get Discovered

You must convince your family members you aren't a thug. Keep any illegal activities completely hidden from the family.

DISCOVERY PENALTY: Automatic Removal

IF you do get discovered (the worst case), you MUST FearRP getting discovered. Family Heads will AUTOMATICALLY ICLY remove you from their branch and trigger a complete removal from the Takeshima Family. We are heavily strict on this!
